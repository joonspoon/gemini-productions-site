Gemini Productions
Projects

Cultural projects by King Davinci and June Clarke.

Rooted in Lagos, carrying Nigerian street dance and culture to the world.

Selected Work

The Playground

Dance Competition

A solo competition in Lagos, built to crown the most complete dance artist rather than the strongest battler. Gender-balanced rounds, performances from Lagos' best in between, and an epic afterparty to close.

Naija Street Style

Workshops, Dance Education

Afro-fusion dance classes led by King Davinci, hosted worldwide, connecting students to the source and history of Nigerian dances so they leave with context and meaning, not just steps.

Eko Freaq

Nightlife

A taste of Lagos nightlife, carried to dancefloors worldwide with Nigerian sound and the energy of home.

Like Water

Touring Duet

A duet between King Davinci and a dancer from the host country, using water's instinct to flow and merge as a metaphor for two cultures meeting and enriching each other.
